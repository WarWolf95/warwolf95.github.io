01 // ANALYTICAL PHILOSOPHY & IMPACT

Core Operating Principles

How I approach data problems: robust dimensional modeling, strict anti-leakage controls, and clear stakeholder delivery.

01

Star Schema Dimensional Modeling

Data must be modeled for analytical clarity and reporting speed. Whether building in SQLite, DuckDB, or MySQL, I structure raw transactions into normalized fact tables and clean dimensional entities, enabling fast Power BI DAX execution without recursive filtering overhead.

02

Strict Anti-Leakage & Governance

In risk scoring and regulatory reporting, using post-origination or unobservable data creates dangerous false confidence. Every pipeline enforces chronological boundary splits and segregates application-time fields from downstream outcomes.

03

Empirical Benchmark Calibration

Internal metrics are only as good as the market context they live in. My work regularly calibrates internal data against published UK government statistics, including FCA General Insurance Value Measures, FOS Ombudsman uphold statistics, and ONS ASHE wage medians.

04

Decision-Ready Delivery

Complex SQL queries and statistical models must translate directly into tools stakeholders can use. I deliver findings through structured Power BI reports, automated executive markdown briefs, and programmatic Excel workbooks with native what-if modeling.
